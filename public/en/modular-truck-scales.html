Split truck scales and load cells

The SCS range offers 20, 30, 50, 60, 80, 100, 120 and 150 tonne capacities. Compare models, installation layouts, applications and load cell specifications.

Match capacity and deck size

The SCS brochure lists 20, 30, 50, 60, 80, 100, 120 and 150 tonne capacities. Check gross vehicle weight, axle spacing, track width and approach direction so every axle to be weighed can stand on the live deck at once.

Compare pit and above-ground layouts

An above-ground deck needs room for approaches. A pit installation needs drainage, cleaning access and space for maintenance. Map turning movements and the straight approach distance when comparing layouts.

Specify compatible load cells

Check rated load, mounting dimensions, output type and indicator compatibility when specifying a cell. Photograph the existing nameplate and wiring arrangement to support compatibility checks and recommissioning.
