Company profile

Established in July 2014, Heyuan Taiheng Top Weighing Equipment Co., Ltd. introduces its history, manufacturing operations and production base.

Company and manufacturing base

Founded in July 2014, Heyuan Taiheng Top Weighing Equipment Co., Ltd. is based in Hengtai Technology Park in Heyuan. Its manufacturing base is in Dalingshan, Dongguan. Confirm the appropriate address when arranging a business meeting or workshop visit.

Define the weighing task

A truck crossing a yard, material travelling on a belt and material stored in a vessel require different load-bearing structures and records. Describe the load, the location and who needs the result before specifying equipment.

Prepare a site brief

Send vehicle photographs, a site plan, available dimensions and the highest expected load. For a retrofit, include the existing indicator model, foundation photographs and a sample ticket so the team can assess what may be retained.
