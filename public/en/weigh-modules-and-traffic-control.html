Weigh modules and access equipment

Compare weigh modules and vehicle access accessories, including card readers, license plate recognition devices, cameras and traffic lights.

Trace the load path

A weigh module transfers a vessel or machine load to its cell. Specify empty weight, maximum contents and support geometry, together with vibration, attached pipework and thermal movement.

Coordinate identification and traffic

Card readers or plate recognition devices identify a vehicle, cameras document the scene and signals direct movement. Mark stopping positions and driver sight lines before locating identification points and signals.

Plan exception handling

Discuss failed plate reads, repeated card scans, reversing vehicles and communications interruptions during design. Document release conditions, who may approve exceptions and which records are retained.
