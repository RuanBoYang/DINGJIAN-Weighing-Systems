Manufacturing and after-sales service

Review the production lines, machining equipment, manufacturing management and after-sales service. The service hotline is 4000-566-765.

Define the manufacturing inputs

Before specifying a truck scale, establish the longest axle-group span, maximum loaded vehicle weight, traffic direction and approach space. Mark drainage, service access and cable routes on the site plan to discuss deck sections, installation layout and indicator position.

Document commissioning

Check delivered deck sections, load cells, junction boxes and indicators against the packing list. Have the commissioning team record zero return, repeat weighings and loading at different deck positions. Keep the wiring diagram and final settings as a maintenance baseline.

Prepare a service request

An unstable empty reading, inconsistent repeat weighings and a communications fault call for different checks. When calling 4000-566-765, provide the model, time of onset, any link with rain or vehicle position, a display photograph and recent maintenance history.
