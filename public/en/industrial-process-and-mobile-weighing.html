Belt, forklift, rail and reactor weighing

See weighing equipment for conveyors, material handling, rail vehicles and chemical reactors, alongside wheel washers and self-service weighing terminals.

Continuous conveyor weighing

A belt scale combines belt loading per unit length with belt speed to calculate flow rate and totalize material throughput. Supply belt width, speed range, inclination, material type and available straight run. Identify how the site will check zero and run a material test.

Forklift and rail applications

For forklift weighing, record load centre, attachment tare and mast position during the weighing step. For rail vehicles, supply track gauge, vehicle type, axle loads and whether weighing takes place at rest or in motion. Give separate layout dimensions for each application.

Load paths in reactor weighing

The vessel, agitator and contents all contribute to the supported load. Pipe restraint, thermal expansion and agitation can affect the reading. Coordinate pipework and weigh module layouts and leave room for cleaning, inspection and calibration.
