Smart mixing plants and customer examples

Explore the smart mixing plant system, its functions and applications, followed by the customer examples listed in the company brochure.

Connect truck and batch records

Incoming material and dispatched product are typically weighed by truck trip, while batching is recorded by ingredient and batch. Link the two record types using order identifiers, material codes and timestamps so receiving and dispatch activity can be traced to production.

Scope equipment by its role

Map aggregate, powder and liquid feeds alongside truck lanes, bins and the control room. Identify which weights support inventory, recipe control or transfer of goods. For each point, define the scale, indicator, interface and procedure for reviewing exceptions.

Compare project references

Compare the materials, vehicle types and operating sequence in the brochure examples with your own site. For a retrofit, provide the installed equipment list, a representative ticket and the specific issue to resolve. These details help establish which assets can be retained and which process steps need to change.
