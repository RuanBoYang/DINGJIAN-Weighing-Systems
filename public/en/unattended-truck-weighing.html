Unattended weighing systems

Review the components, vehicle weighing workflow and management functions of an unattended weighbridge, illustrated by a site installation.

A complete unattended weighing cycle

After vehicle identification, confirm that the entire vehicle is on the deck and capture a stable indicator reading. Match gross and tare to the same trip to calculate net weight. Lane direction determines where identification devices, displays and driver prompts belong.

When the operator takes over

Unreadable plates, tailgating, an unstable weight and a missing tare each need a designated operator and holding point. Define the driver prompt, conditions for manual release and the event record for each exception.

Size the lane for peak traffic

Record peak arrivals and departures, maximum vehicle length, queue space and the direction of empty and loaded traffic. Allow time for stopping, identification, weight capture and departure when comparing shared lanes, directional lanes and a separate review bay.
