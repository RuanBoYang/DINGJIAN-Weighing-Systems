Livestock scales, feed bins and rail scales

Explore slaughterhouse rail scales, feed bin weighing systems and livestock scales, with equipment and installation photographs.

Weighing live animals

Animals move while on the platform. Define whether each weighing cycle covers one animal or a group, then record the maximum combined weight, pen dimensions and gate direction. Discuss how the indicator accepts a stable reading and how the cleaning routine affects deck finish and load cell protection.

Measuring feed bin inventory

Weigh modules carry the bin and its contents. An empty-bin reference allows operators to track deliveries and consumption by weight. Specify the number of legs, uneven loading, wind exposure and pipe connections when planning the supports and calibration access.

Weighing on an overhead rail

A suspended load transfers its weight through the hanger to the weighing rail. Provide the rail profile, hanger tare, hanger spacing, line speed and washdown requirements so the supplier can assess the section length, tare procedure and point of capture.
