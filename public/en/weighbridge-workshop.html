Facilities and production workshop

See the entrance, offices, factory grounds and the workshops used to fabricate and weld scale platforms.

Inspect the deck assembly

The brochure shows the grounds, fabrication area and welding workshop. Bring vehicle wheelbase information and an installation drawing to review module joints, service clearances and load cell supports.

Plan delivery and lifting

Gate width, turning space, unloading position and lifting access affect how deck sections reach their final positions. Mark these constraints on the site plan before agreeing section sizes and the delivery sequence.

Check interfaces before delivery

Use the agreed drawing to check deck dimensions, module identifiers, cable exits and junction box locations. Record the drawing revision that the installation team will use.
