Company qualifications and honors

Inspect the certificates and honors reproduced in the company brochure. Open the full-size spread for details.

Identify the certificate holder

Compare the legal name, issuing body and certificate number shown in the brochure with the proposed contracting party. Where manufacturing, sales and installation involve different parties, record each party’s role.

Check scope and dates

Review the activity, location and validity period covered by each document. A brochure records information at publication. Request a current, legible copy when preparing tender or acceptance documents.

Build the procurement file

List corporate documents, model-specific evidence and site handover records separately. Assign a submission stage to each item so gaps can be identified while quotations are being prepared.
