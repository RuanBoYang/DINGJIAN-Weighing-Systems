Unattended weighbridge systems

Identify the vehicle by card or plate at entry. Position sensors check the truck on the deck before the indicator captures a stable weight and cameras record the load. After loading or unloading, the second weight is matched to the first for ticketing and release. Lanes can operate in one or both directions.

Industrial weighing applications

Ports and aggregates yards can link truck weighing to conveyor or loader measurements. Ready-mix plants track deliveries by contract and concrete grade. Feed silos use support modules to monitor inventory, while reactor systems measure vessel loads at their supports.
